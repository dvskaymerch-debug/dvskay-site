Delfino Vieira Nsuka, known to the world as DVskay, is an Angolan-born artist, rapper, and producer. Born on September 26, 1999 in Luanda, Angola, he moved to the United States in 2017 at the age of 17 — carrying a dream and a voice that couldn't be silenced.

He started making music with friends in 2017 at age 17, and by 2019 he officially dropped his first track "While You Vibin" — the response was immediate. Since then, he hasn't stopped. His sound blends RAP, RnB, and Afro influences into something raw and unmistakably his own — the kind of music that hits different because it comes from a real place.

"I just want to expand my music with y'all and be a better person, artist everyday. Follow me, and let's make this journey fun and legendary."

With singles like "Deep End," "Pray," "Vanilla," and his EP "Roots" under Skay Records, DVskay continues to push his craft on his own terms — building something that lasts longer than a trend cycle.
